PIETOOLS 2026 Release Notes

NOTE: [availability line, as in the 2024 and 2025 notes; to be added at release]

PIETOOLS 2026 represents PI operators in any number of spatial variables, including operators between
spaces of different dimension, and adds SOS programming for nonlinear PIEs (PIESOS). New classes replace
the PI operator classes of PIETOOLS 2025 (opvar, dopvar, opvar2d, dopvar2d); the functions that used
those classes have new names, listed under Minor Updates.

Major Updates:

1. Classes for PI operators in any number of spatial variables (folder sopvar).
-- copvar (fixed operators) and cdopvar (operators that depend on decision variables) replace opvar,
   dopvar, opvar2d and dopvar2d. One copvar or cdopvar object maps a concatenation of spaces R^n and
   L2[s], with s any set of spatial variables, to another such concatenation; one operator can
   therefore couple states of different dimension, e.g. on R^n x L2[s1] x L2[s1,s2].
-- Each block of a copvar (cdopvar) object is a sopvar (sdopvar) object: a PI operator from
   L2[S1,S3] to L2[S2,S3], for sets S1, S2, S3 of spatial variables.
-- Operations: +, -, * (composition), ' (adjoint), .', concatenation, blkdiag, diff, int, subs, ==,
   and inv (operators in one spatial variable; in several variables, separable operators).
-- Constructors: mat2copvar_sop (multiplication by a matrix), eye_copvar_sop (identity),
   zeros_copvar_sop (zero operator), pi4_copvar_sop (4-PI operator from its parameters P, Q1, Q2, R),
   int2copvar (integral operator from its kernel).
-- opvar2copvar and pie2copvar convert opvar objects, and PIEs whose operators are opvar objects,
   to the new classes.
-- The folders opvar, lpi_programming, executives and settings of PIETOOLS 2025 are removed.

2. LPI programming with the new classes (folder lpi_programming_sopvar).
-- poscopvar declares positive operators and lpivar_cdopvar free operators; lpi_ineq_sop and
   lpi_eq_sop impose operator inequalities and equalities; getsol_lpivar_sop and lpigetsol_sop
   return the solved values.
-- An operator can stand for the dimensions, spaces and domain of a declaration:
   poscopvar(prog,T,deg) declares P on the output spaces of T (T' for its input spaces),
   lpivar_cdopvar(prog,B',deg) declares Z with the spaces of B', and eye_copvar_sop(P) is the
   identity on the spaces of P. The forms with dimensions, spaces and domain remain.
-- The LPI program carries its settings: lpiprogram(vars,dom,tier), or lpisetsettings(prog,settings)
   after declaration. With DEG omitted, poscopvar and lpivar_cdopvar take their degrees from these
   settings; lpi_ineq_sop takes from them the options a call does not give.

3. One executive per problem, for PIEs with copvar operators (folder executives_sopvar).
-- PIETOOLS_stability_sop(PIE,settings,form) replaces PIETOOLS_PDEstability, PIETOOLS_PIE2PDEstability,
   their dual versions, PIETOOLS_stability_2D and PIETOOLS_stability_dual_2D.
-- PIETOOLS_Hinf_gain_sop(PIE,settings,form,gain) replaces PIETOOLS_Hinf_gain and its coercive, dual
   and 2D versions (7 files).
-- PIETOOLS_H2_norm_sop(PIE,settings,form) replaces PIETOOLS_H2_norm_c, PIETOOLS_H2_norm_o and their
   coercive and 2D versions (8 files).
-- PIETOOLS_Hinf_estimator_sop (also replacing PIETOOLS_Hinf_estimator_2D), PIETOOLS_Hinf_control_sop,
   PIETOOLS_H2_estimator_sop, PIETOOLS_H2_control_sop, PIETOOLS_well_posedness_sop,
   PIETOOLS_auto_execute_sop and lpiscript_sop replace the PIETOOLS 2025 functions of the same name
   without the suffix _sop. getController_sop and getObserver_sop replace getController, getObserver
   and getObserver_2D.
-- Stability, H-infinity gain and H2 norm accept PIEs in any number of spatial variables. The
   H-infinity estimator returns its gain for PIEs in one or two spatial variables. The H-infinity
   and H2 controllers, the H2 estimator and the well-posedness test accept PIEs in one spatial
   variable.
-- New: PIETOOLS_Hinf_OFcontrol_sop computes a dynamic output-feedback controller, itself a PIE, and a
   bound on the L2 gain from w to z of the closed loop. It requires D22 = 0, no inputs at the
   boundary and no algebraic constraint K. DEMO13 illustrates it.

4. Settings (folder settings_sopvar).
-- settings_sopvar_stripped, _light, _heavy, _veryheavy, _extreme and _custom replace
   settings_PIETOOLS_<tier> and settings_PIETOOLS_<tier>_2D: one file per tier, with entries for each
   number of spatial variables. lpisettings_sop(tier) replaces lpisettings.

5. Conversion of PDEs.
-- The converter accepts PDEs in any number of spatial variables, periodic boundary conditions
   (represented by the constraint K x = 0 on the PIE state, PIE.K), and PDEs that couple states on
   domains of different dimension. The converter of PIETOOLS 2025 remains, as
   converters/legacy/convert_PIETOOLS_PDE_2D.

6. Nonlinear PDEs and PIEs (folder PIESOS).
-- Terms of a PDE can be multiplied and raised to powers (times, power, mpower), which declares
   polynomial nonlinear PDEs. A nonlinear PIE is an npie_struct object; pie2npie_struct and
   npie2pie_struct convert between the linear and nonlinear PIE structures.
-- PIESOS poses SOS programs for nonlinear PIEs: the distributed polynomial classes polyopvar,
   tensopvar, tensopmat, intop and quadPoly; the program functions piesos_program, piesos_sosvar,
   piesos_polyvar, piesos_LF, piesos_ineq, piesos_eq, piesos_setobj, piesos_solve and piesos_getsol;
   Liediff, the Lie derivative of a distributed functional along solutions of a PIE; and the
   executives PIESOS_stability_global, PIESOS_stability_local and PIESOS_sublevel.
-- Demos PIESOS_DEMO1_global_stability, PIESOS_DEMO2_local_stability and PIESOS_DEMO3_energy_bound,
   and the library examples_PDE_library_PIESOS.

7. Demos and examples.
-- DEMO10: decay rate of a PDE with periodic boundary conditions. DEMO11: Poincare constant of a 2D
   domain. DEMO12: state-feedback stabilization of a 2D reaction-diffusion PDE. DEMO13: H-infinity
   optimal dynamic output feedback. DEMO14 and DEMO15: trace inequalities on [0,1] and on the unit
   square.
-- DEMO1 to DEMO9 use the new classes and the _sop functions.
-- New examples: examples_FI_library_PIETOOLS, a library of functional inequalities whose constants
   are computed by LPIs; a 2D plate and a damped 2D wave equation; three 3D examples; 16 examples that
   couple states on domains of different dimension; and 13 command-line examples (l2gain_23,
   l2gain_26, stability_13_2, stability_13_3, stability_16_1, stability_16_3, stability_16_4,
   stability_17, stability_18, stability_19, stability_36, stability_39, stability_42).

Minor Updates:

1. Functions replaced or renamed (PIETOOLS 2025 -> PIETOOLS 2026).
-- opvar, opvar2d -> copvar;  dopvar, dopvar2d -> cdopvar
-- poslpivar, poslpivar_2d -> poscopvar
-- lpivar, lpivar_2d -> lpivar_cdopvar
-- lpi_ineq, lpi_ineq_2d -> lpi_ineq_sop
-- lpi_eq, lpi_eq_2d -> lpi_eq_sop
-- lpigetsol -> lpigetsol_sop;  getsol_lpivar, getsol_lpivar_2d -> getsol_lpivar_sop
-- lpiscript -> lpiscript_sop
-- lpisettings -> lpisettings_sop;  settings_PIETOOLS_<tier>, settings_PIETOOLS_<tier>_2D ->
   settings_sopvar_<tier>
-- mat2opvar -> mat2copvar_sop
-- getController -> getController_sop;  getObserver, getObserver_2D -> getObserver_sop
-- the executives -> one executive per problem with the suffix _sop (Major Update 3)
-- lpiprogram, lpidecvar, lpisetobj and lpisolve keep their names.

2. PIESIM: 13 operator discretization routines have new names, e.g. PIESIM_PIop_discretize ->
   PIESIM_PI_discretize, PIESIM_3PI2Mat_cheb_opint_discretize -> PIESIM_3PI_opint_discretize and, in
   2D, PIESIM_9PI2Mat_cheb_opint_discretize_area -> PIESIM_9PI_opint_area_discretize_2D;
   PIESIM_disturbance_check holds the disturbance check formerly in PIESIM_input_check. These
   routines are internal to PIESIM.
