The present tiers cap the kernel monomials of a basis operator by a total degree: \code{joint} over the $2N$ variables of the $N$-D entry, and hand-written pair tables in the 2-D tier files (Section~\ref{sec:cannot}). A total-degree cap does not factor over the directions, which is why the light and heavy stock 2-D storages keep incomparable pair sets, and a pair table has to be rewritten for every length. The replacement is one cap per direction: for a basis operator of a space and a direction $k$ the space owns, every kernel monomial satisfies $\deg_{\theta_k}+\deg_{s_k}\le c_k$; in a direction the space does not own, $\deg_{\theta_k}\le c_k$. The constraint of direction $k$ involves only the two variables of that direction, so the set of kept monomials is the product over directions of the 1-D sets, the basis of the $N$-D entry is the tensor product of capped 1-D bases, and a tier whose caps are entrywise no smaller than another's keeps every monomial the other keeps. At the light lengths of a 2-D entry ($d_2=2$, $d_{3\bb}=2$: $\theta_k,s_k\in\{0,1\}$) the cap $c_k=2$ keeps all four pairs of direction $k$ ($16$ of $16$ in the complete-moment block), the cap $c_k=1$ keeps $1$, $\theta_k$, $s_k$ ($9$ of $16$), and the cap $c_k=0$ the multiplier alone; the stock light table keeps $7$ of $16$ and the stock heavy table $9$ (Table~\ref{tab:2d}), neither a product set. The cap is the field \code{cap} ($1\times N_{\mathrm{reg}}$) of a degree specification of \code{poscopvar\_direct}, applied as a subset array over $[\theta_1,\dots,\theta_{N_{\mathrm{reg}}},s_{\mathrm{own}}]$ after the multiplier caps and before the exponent grid is built (the shadow in \file{mixed_domain/harness}; the repository's \code{subset} array stays available for a full-registry list); in the row it is the field \code{cap}, one entry per direction of the entry, $\infty$ for none, and the reader fills the directions a space does not own from the entry of the largest owning space.

Measured in Sections~\ref{sec:bl-cap} and \ref{sec:bl-mixed}: the cap at $2$ reproduces the uncapped all-pattern light row exactly on four rows (same variables, rows, values and verdicts), and the cap at $1$ returns that row to the stock size on every row of the 2-D library, the 2-D ladder and the mixed suite with the stock verdicts, at $6\%$ of the fin2 gap and with the mixed gains between the stock and the uncapped row.
