\subsection{What closed them}\label{sec:gaps-closed}

\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item \textbf{The degree of the free operator.} Q2 closed G1, G2, FO2r and FO2s in the direct form at $\mathrm{dw}=0$. At degree $1$ no slack change closed S17, FO2r or FO2s: the uniform raises $\mathrm{dw}=1$ and $2$ returned U or out. On FO2r and FO2s Q2 closes without a weighted term ($281\,497$ and $235\,353$, $7\%$ and $5\%$ below Q2 with faces1 and \code{wcross} $0$, $302\,957$ and $248\,497$); on S17 and S18 the plain term alone at Q2 does not certify. On the 3-D heat the executive direct form with Q2 keeps the slack at its size, grows the equality rows from $25\,741$ to $107\,857$, and is infeasible.
\item \textbf{The offset-1 faces.} Wherever a smaller term set certified in the direct form it was faces1. At the same degree of the free operator and the same \code{wcross}, faces0, the choice of \code{'auto'} at $w=1$, costs $3.7\times$ on G3 ($210\,340$ against $56\,548$) and $4.0\times$ on G1, G2, FO2r and FO2s at Q2 (S18 $940\,188$ against $235\,488$; S17 $1\,160\,588$ against $290\,588$). Against faces1 with \code{wcross} $0$ the ratios are $4.3$ (G1), $4.4$ (G2), $4.6$ (FO2r) and $4.7$ (FO2s).
\item \textbf{The heavy lengths in the storage form.} The 2-D storage with the first row of $d_3$ raised from $1$ to $2$ (the heavy lengths, uniform or separable) closed G3 at the light slack ($188\,320$; $104\,080$ with \code{dw\_feas(2)} $0$) and FO2r at $0.1\tau^*$ ($532\,276$ with \code{dw\_feas(2)} $0$). The veryheavy 2-D lengths closed G2 ($953\,001$ with \code{dw\_feas(2)} $0$, against $1\,512\,873$ at the light slack). With the stock or the light 2-D storage no slack change closed a storage-form gap: four term sets, \code{dw\_feas} up to $3$ and \code{wcross} $0$ were infeasible or U on G2 and G3.
\item \textbf{The per-space raise.} Raising the weight of the 1-D space of S18 alone (\code{dw\_space} $[1\ 0]$) certifies G2 at $93\,991$ variables with faces1, against $440\,023$ for the uniform raise; through the repository files $65\,731$ with \code{wcross} $0$ (work package 6). No per-space raise closed S17, FO2s or a storage-form gap.
\end{enumerate}
The cross weight \code{wcross} $0$ lost no certificate in the gap study and cut $8$ to $28\%$ of the variables on every multi-space case (\file{gap_results.md}, \file{perspace_results.md}). In the term experiments of 11/16 it turned one certificate, S17 at $0.5r_2^*$ with faces0, into U (Section~\ref{sec:program}).

\subsection{The hidden choices of \code{'auto'}}\label{sec:gaps-auto}

\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item \textbf{The offset switch.} \code{'auto'} takes the faces at offset $0$ when some $w_k<2$ and at offset $1$ otherwise. In two variables at $w=1$ this choice cost $3.6$ to $4.7\times$ the variables wherever offset $1$ certified (Section~\ref{sec:gaps-closed}; PDE~33 $190\,900$ against $52\,660$). Offset $0$ is not dominated: on S18 at $0.5\tau^*$ with $\mathrm{dw}=0$ and the free operator at degree $1$ only faces0 certifies (Table~\ref{tab:t2}); under the implemented tier \code{'faces1'} certifies it after one raise. On TL at $0.9\lambda^*$ offset $0$ certifies and \code{'faces1'} is UNKNOWN (Section~\ref{sec:gaps-phasec}). In three variables offset $1$ is infeasible on the 3-D heat at $658\,536$ variables, so offset $0$ is needed there, as on 11/15. The tiers take offset $1$ in two variables and the rule in one and three (\file{DECISIONS.md}, item D72).
\item \textbf{The degree of the free operator} is not a choice of \code{'auto'}: it is \code{lpi.var.deg}, read by the direct form at the entry of the number of variables of the PIE. It is the knob that closed four gaps (Section~\ref{sec:gaps-closed}).
\item \textbf{The per-space degree rule.} Reading the support of the slack per space, in the directions the space owns, returned the registry degrees on every case (S3, S17, S18, the population, FO2s, the 2-D heat). The rule is inert on this suite and was not ported (\file{DECISIONS.md}, item D69).
\item \textbf{The storage form.} No choice of \code{'auto'} mattered: on G2 and G3 no slack configuration with the stock or light storage certified, up to $1\,723\,689$ variables.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The executive after the day}\label{sec:gaps-exec}

\item \textbf{The slack options.} \code{get\_lift\_degs} implements \code{wcross}, \code{dw\_space} and the named set \code{'faces1'}; \code{lpi\_ineq\_sop} merges \code{wcross} and \code{dw\_space} from \code{lpi.ineq} and accepts \code{psatz} as a per-dimension cell (work package 6, \file{scratchpad/wp6/wp6_verify.log}). Verification through the repository files: the default output is unchanged on S3 and S18; \code{'faces1'} realizes the codes $[0\ 3\ 4\ 5\ 6]$ at offsets $[0\ 1\ 1\ 1\ 1]$ in two variables and $[0\ 1]$ at $[0\ 1]$ in one; a \code{dw\_space} of the wrong length is an error; S18 at $0.9\tau^*$ certifies at $93\,991$ variables and at $65\,731$ with \code{wcross} $0$. The per-space degree rule is not ported.
\item \textbf{The tiers.} The six tier files carry the row, \code{wcross} $0$ and \code{psatz} \code{\{'auto','faces1','auto'\}} (Section~\ref{sec:knobs-slack}, paragraph Implemented 11/17/2026). \code{settings\_2d} is out of the tiers; the stock 2-D construction is \code{settings\_2d\_stock(level)}, identical to the former tier section in $18$ of $18$ comparisons (work package 7; \file{DECISIONS.md}, item D74).
\item \textbf{The examples.} Cases $56$ to $72$ of \code{examples\_PDE\_library\_PIETOOLS} hold the mixed-domain and literature systems; five of them return the settings that certify them (Table~\ref{tab:app-settings}).
