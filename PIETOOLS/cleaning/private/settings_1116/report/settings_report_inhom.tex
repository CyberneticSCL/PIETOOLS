\subsection{Variable coefficients}\label{sec:bl-inhom}

Three 2-D systems on $[0,1]^2$ with homogeneous Dirichlet conditions on all four edges and a constant reaction coefficient $\lambda$ test polynomial coefficients in a single domain:
\begin{align*}
\text{A:}\quad & x_t = (1+s_1^2)\,x_{s_1s_1} + (1+s_2)\,x_{s_2s_2} + \lambda x,\\
\text{B:}\quad & x_t = x_{s_1s_1} + x_{s_2s_2} + (\lambda - 3s_1s_2)\,x,\\
\text{C:}\quad & x_t = (1+s_1)\,x_{s_1s_1} + x_{s_2s_2} + s_2\,x_{s_1} + \lambda x.
\end{align*}
The cascade M couples $x_1(s_1)$ on $[0,1]$ to $x_2(s_1,s_2)$ on $[0,1]^2$ through the edge $s_2=0$:
\begin{align*}
& x_{1,t} = (1+s_1)\,x_{1,s_1s_1} + r_1 x_1, \qquad x_{2,t} = x_{2,s_1s_1} + (1+s_2^2)\,x_{2,s_2s_2} + r_2 x_2,\\
& x_1(0)=x_1(1)=0,\quad x_2(0,s_2)=x_2(1,s_2)=0,\quad x_2(s_1,0)=x_1(s_1),\quad x_2(s_1,1)=0,
\end{align*}
with $r_1 = 14.507976\,f$ and $r_2 = 22.205980\,f$ at the fraction $f$. Second-order finite differences with $N$ interior nodes per direction give the thresholds $\lambda^*$: A $26.839174$ ($N=40$) and $26.846533$ ($N=60$); B $20.474475$ and $20.479775$; C $24.242392$ and $24.248789$; the 2-D state of M $22.199946$ and $22.205980$; the 1-D state of M $14.504004$ and $14.507976$. The $N=60$ values are the reference for every reach fraction below. The values rise with $N$, so the reach fractions are upper bounds; a Richardson estimate (inferred, assuming an $O(h^2)$ error) lowers each reach by $0.02$--$0.03\%$. The threshold of the cascade in $f$ is $1$ (inferred from the triangular coupling; not computed). Every run is \code{PIETOOLS\_stability\_sop} in form \code{'P'} at the light tier unless stated; Rlight0 is the stock light lengths with the \code{separable} pattern set in two variables; bisections take $8$ steps on $[0.5,1]$; records in \file{sopvar/psatz_faces_results/mixed_domain/inhom_results.md}.

On the library example PDE~7 (Gahlawat 2017) the two storages give identical programs ($568$ variables, $75$ rows, dw $1$) and verdicts at $\lambda=4.0$ and $4.5$. The stock bisection on $[3,4.66]$ certified every point up to $4.653516$ at light and heavy, $0.99861$ of the $4.66$ of the library header and $0.99994$ of the $N=200$ reference $4.653780$; no value above $4.653516$ was tested. On PDE~23 (Shivakumar 2019) at $\lambda=4.6$ the container executive \code{PIETOOLS\_Hinf\_gain\_sop} gives the gain $23.7057$ at light, against $25.249$ in the library header, and $23.7058$ at heavy, against $23.706$; the stock \code{lpiscript} path was not run.

On A, B and C the two storages returned the same verdict and the same dw at all $9$ values of $\lambda$ per system and the same certified $\lambda$, with Rlight0 larger by $561$ variables ($0.3\%$) at dw $1$. On the cascade M the storages differ: at $f=0.9$ stock light returned U after one raise and Rlight0 light certified, and the reach is $0.73633$ for stock light against $0.97656$ for Rlight0 light, at $508\,228$ against $510\,057$ variables. Stock heavy certified $f=0.9$ with $1\,023\,444$ variables, $2.01$ times the Rlight0 light program, in $76$~s. Every non-certified run returned U; none returned infeasible. In the direct test every term set certified M and A at the fraction $0.9$, so these runs do not separate the term sets; relative to facesS at dws $0$ the variable counts are $3.6$ times for faces0, $5.9$ times for box at dws $1$ and $6.6$--$6.7$ times for product2. No bisection of the direct-test term sets and no Rlight0 run at the heavy tier was done.
