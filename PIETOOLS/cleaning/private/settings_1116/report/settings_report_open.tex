\item \textbf{Terms per block on a mixed registry.} The slack on $L_2[s_1]\oplus L_2[s_1,s_2]$ is one positive operator whose sub-operators between pairs of spaces have different kernel variables, and the construction applies one registry weight per term to every sub-operator, the one on $L_2[s_1]$ included, which absorbs $s_2$ through the registry monomials of the direction it does not own. This is a rule of the implementation, measured sufficient on the mixed suite (Tables~\ref{tab:t1}, \ref{tab:t2}, \ref{tab:t3}: the registry faces in every direction, products across directions adding nothing) and backed by no theorem: the Positivstellens\"atze of Section~\ref{sec:terms} are stated for one polynomial ring. A per-sub-operator construction, one PSD block per term and per sub-operator, the $L_2[s_1]$ sub-operator weighted by the faces of $s_1$ only and the coupling sub-operators by the union, is the design step that would address mixed domains; \code{poscopvar\_direct} builds one Gram per term today, and the 3-D cost measurement of the degree raise (job 1706087 on Sol) does not bear on it.
