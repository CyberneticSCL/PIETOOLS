\begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item \textbf{The revision of \code{poscopvar}.} The reader of Section~\ref{sec:reader-new} and the per-direction cap live in scratch shadows (\file{sopvar/psatz_faces_results/mixed_domain/harness}: \code{pf\_row\_storage.m}; the \code{cap} field of \code{poscopvar\_direct}, a subset array over $[\theta,s_{\mathrm{own}}]$ for every space). Three changes to the repository follow from them: the omitted-\code{DEG} rule of \code{poscopvar} (every direction of a 2-D space separable, two operators per space) is replaced by the row; the lift enumerates $\{1,2,3\}$ or $\{1,4\}$ per direction, so a pattern set with both $\bb$ and $\bc$ in one direction is read as lower plus upper, which equals lower plus complete only at equal multiplier lengths of the two blocks (Proposition~\ref{prop:span}); and the per-direction cap replaces \code{joint} and the 2-D cap tables. \code{poscopvar\_lift}, the explicit $\mcl A^*\mcl M\mcl A$ of which \code{poscopvar} is the efficient implementation, is back on the path (\file{lpi_programming_sopvar/misc}, with \code{posmult\_cdopvar}; a working-tree move) as the analysis form of every row.
\item \textbf{The tier files.} Done on 11/17/2026 (Section~\ref{sec:knobs-slack}, paragraph Implemented 11/17/2026; \file{DECISIONS.md}, items D73, D74): the storage row replaces \code{mult}, \code{int}, \code{joint}, \code{sep} and \code{settings\_2d}, whose stock construction survives as \code{settings\_2d\_stock(level)}; \code{keepdual}, \code{witness}, \code{N\_cheb} and \code{nfreq} are defaults of \code{exec\_sop\_settings} and \code{lpisettings\_sop}. One change remains: \code{lpi\_ineq\_sop} takes \code{psatz\_rows} and \code{psatz\_rows\_offset} from the call only, so a tier cannot carry a term set other than the named ones.
\item \textbf{The default test in the executives.} Done for stability: the \code{'Q'} form of \code{PIETOOLS\_stability\_sop} is the direct form, sized from its own support at base $0$ with the free operator at \code{lpi.var.deg} (Section~\ref{sec:exec-settings}). Its forms \code{'P'}, \code{'Qd'} and \code{'FQ'} and the other executives are unchanged.
\item \textbf{Reach near the margin.} Closed by application-specific settings (Table~\ref{tab:app-settings}; \gapsref): S17 at $0.9r_2^*$ and S18 at $0.9\tau^*$ certify in the direct form with the free operator at degree $2$, and S18 also with the per-space raise.
\item \textbf{The raise ladder of the executive.} Measured: the degree of the free operator closes S17, FO2r and FO2s, where uniform slack raises of one and two degrees return UNKNOWN or infeasible, and closes S18 at $215\,188$ variables against $440\,023$ for the uniform raise; the per-space raise of the 1-D space closes S18 at $93\,991$ against $440\,023$ (\gapsref). The executive raises only the slack weight, uniformly. A ladder ``free-operator degree, then term offset $0$, then slack weight, lowest-dimension spaces first'' is a proposal, not implemented and not run as a ladder in the executive; computed from the rows of the Phase C sweep, the direct-test ladder C1 $\to$ C2 $\to$ C0 (faces1 at degree $1$, then Q2, then offset $0$ at degree $1$) certifies $24$ of $29$ cases at $10.48$M variables summed over the rungs tried, against $21.40$M for C0 $\to$ C4 (Table~\ref{tab:phasec-ladder}). The offset-0 step is there for the two cases the 2-D entry \code{'faces1'} hands to the raise loop: S18 at $0.5\tau^*$, certified by offset $0$ at $\mathrm{dw}=0$ and by \code{'faces1'} after one raise, and TL at $0.9\lambda^*$, certified by offset $0$ at $1\,152\,400$ variables and UNKNOWN under \code{'faces1'} at $288\,400$, a raise not measured (Section~\ref{sec:gaps-phasec}).
\item \textbf{The direct form behind the storage form.} S2 (at $a=0.5$), S6, S7, S9 at $0.9$ of their margins and the Laplace--Beltrami edge case at $0.9\lambda^*$ are infeasible in the direct form under every candidate of the Phase C sweep (Section~\ref{sec:gaps-phasec}) and are certified by the storage form; S17, S18, the population, FO2r and FO2s are the reverse (Table~\ref{tab:complement}). On S6 and S7 the slack raise $\mathrm{dw}=1$ and Q2 with faces0 are infeasible as well, and Q2 with $\mathrm{dw}=1$ exceeds the cap; no ladder of the direct form below the cap certifies them (Section~\ref{sec:gaps-phasec}). A combined default test, the direct form first and the storage form on failure, or the reverse, is a design decision for the maintainer. Its cost is that of the two programs, at the measured light sizes for example S3 $84\,205$ (\code{'P'}) and $86\,078$ (\code{'Q'}), S8 $244\,521$ and $101\,424$, PDE~33 $26\,545$ and $52\,660$.
\item \textbf{The 3-D row count of the light \code{'P'} program.} The 3-D heat at $0.9\kappa^*$ under the implemented light tier has $m=37\,114$ equality rows against $23\,547$ in the record, at $+0.4\%$ variables ($911\,425$ against $907\,729$), with the same verdict. Not investigated.
\item \textbf{The demos after the removal of \code{settings\_2d}.} \code{DEMO12} was checked for its storage size only (the row $3\,721$ against stock $3\,721$ variables on PDE~1); \code{heat2d\_compare}, \code{set2d\_deg} and \code{pielr\_lpi} read \code{settings\_2d\_stock(level)} and pass \code{checkcode}. \item \textbf{The help of the executives.} Nine executives (\code{PIETOOLS\_stability\_sop}, \code{\_well\_posedness\_sop}, \code{\_Hinf\_gain\_sop}, \code{\_H2\_norm\_sop}, \code{\_Hinf\_estimator\_sop}, \code{\_H2\_estimator\_sop}, \code{\_Hinf\_control\_sop}, \code{\_H2\_control\_sop}, \code{\_Hinf\_OFcontrol\_sop}) still list the 1-D stock fields \code{dd1}, \code{dd12}, \code{options1}, \code{options12} and \code{override1} as settings, which no tier carries; the stability and well-posedness help also lists \code{eppos\_stab}, removed on 11/09/2026.
\item \textbf{Three variables.} The 3-D entries carry the uniform set and $d_1=0$ (today's N-D entry, uncapped: $65$ Gram rows per component against $23$ under the \code{joint} cap, $0.4\%$ of the variables of the 3-D heat benchmark); the separable set at $(2,2,2,2)$ costs $136$ rows against $65$ and \code{all} $1000$ (Table~\ref{tab:size}), and no 3-D row of the mixed kind has a baseline. The stability executive at light is infeasible on the 3-D heat at $0.9\kappa^*$ under every storage (Table~\ref{tab:bl-fin2}), with the slack bases bounded by the degree of $\mcl T$ (\code{cap\_delta} $0$) and no raise allowed; without the bound the same slack has $20.9$M decision variables ($m=126\,681$; stopped after $20$~min at $49$~GB), so the bound is what keeps the 3-D light program at $908$k and a raise within it is the question. The heat benchmark record (Cor.~35 of the lift notes; faces at offset $0$) certifies $0.985\kappa^*$ with its own LPI, and the box quadratics one weight degree up in the slack alone certify $0.985\kappa^*$ on Sol at $13.9$M variables in $5.1$~h on $64$ threads (job 1706087), $5.1\times$ the variables of the full-length faces at the lower degree, while the run which also raised the free operator did not finish in $9.5$~h. The 3-D slack settings of the light tier are the open item, not the storage. The gap study of 11/17/2026 closed neither 3-D case under the cap of $3$M variables (\gapsref). The 3-D heat at $0.9\kappa^*$ (G5): the direct form with the offset-1 faces fits at $658\,536$ variables and is infeasible; the free operator at degree $2$ leaves the slack at its size and is infeasible as well ($666\,797$); \code{'P'} with the faces at offset $0$ at $\mathrm{dw}=2$ is infeasible at $1\,306\,897$. The 2-D delayed heat with a 3-D registry at $0.5\tau^*$ (G6, D2): the direct form has at least $5\,892\,828$ variables, above the cap, and \code{'P'} raised to $\mathrm{dw}=2$ is infeasible at $2\,514\,256$.
\item \textbf{The stock heavy block.} The row at the heavy lengths reproduces the size of the stock heavy 2-D storage and not its fin2 gap ($3.7\times10^{-5}$ against $1.5$--$1.9\times10^{-4}$): the cap tables of \code{settings\_2d} admit monomials that lengths and a per-direction cap do not express (Section~\ref{sec:cannot}). Which lengths reproduce that gap, if any below the veryheavy entry, is unmeasured; the stock 2-D storage stays reachable through the explicit degree argument of \code{poscopvar}, outside the settings (Section~\ref{sec:app-settings}), and as \code{settings\_2d\_stock(level)}. Unchanged by the work of 11/17/2026.
\item \textbf{Solver variation on the gains.} Certified gains on fin2 and on PDE~48 are not monotone in nested families at the $10^{-4}$ level (two inverted pairs on fin2; PDE~48 under the per-direction cap below the uncapped row), so a gain ranks rows only beyond a factor $1.3$, and the estimator values are bounds.
\item \textbf{Terms per block on a mixed registry.} The slack on $L_2[s_1]\oplus L_2[s_1,s_2]$ is one positive operator whose sub-operators between pairs of spaces have different kernel variables, and the construction applies one registry weight per term to every sub-operator, the one on $L_2[s_1]$ included, which absorbs $s_2$ through the registry monomials of the direction it does not own. This is a rule of the implementation, measured sufficient on the mixed suite (Tables~\ref{tab:t1}, \ref{tab:t2}, \ref{tab:t3}: the registry faces in every direction, products across directions adding nothing) and backed by no theorem: the Positivstellens\"atze of Section~\ref{sec:terms} are stated for one polynomial ring. A per-sub-operator construction, one PSD block per term and per sub-operator, the $L_2[s_1]$ sub-operator weighted by the faces of $s_1$ only and the coupling sub-operators by the union, is the design step that would address mixed domains; \code{poscopvar\_direct} builds one Gram per term today, and the 3-D cost measurement of the degree raise (job 1706087 on Sol) does not bear on it.
\item \textbf{The conjecture.} Necessity of the moment representation (Conjecture~\ref{conj}) is unproven in one variable and a further conjecture in $N$; the Poincar\'e operator is proven outside the cone without the box weight at every length, and the mixed-domain lift $\gamma I-\mcl T^*\mcl T$ reaches the sharp constant to $2\times10^{-5}$ with the faces at the light lengths. Nothing in this report proves that a row certifies every positive operator; it records which rows certify which baselines.
\end{enumerate}
