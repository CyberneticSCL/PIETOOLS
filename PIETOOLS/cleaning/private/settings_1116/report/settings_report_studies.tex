\subsection{Soundness near a margin}\label{sec:st-sound}

On FO2s the direct form with $\mathrm{dD}=0$ and $\mcl Q$ raised to degree $2$ passes both gates at $\tau=2.88253$. The exact margin is $\tau^*=2.8814005$, from the first mode $\lambda^2+2\lambda+0.1+e^{-\lambda\tau}=0$, and the rightmost eigenvalue at the certified $\tau$ has real part $+6.8\times10^{-5}$ (D123, D125). Its optimality measure ($0.867$) and relative residual ($8.6\times10^{-4}$) lie within the ranges of the four stable certified steps of the bisection ($0.810$ to $1.109$; $5.4$ to $9.5\times10^{-4}$), and its $\eta$ ($1.5\times10^{-8}$) exceeds their largest ($1.0\times10^{-8}$) by a factor $1.5$, against a gate of $10^{-7}$. One step further, at the growth rate $1.7\times10^{-4}$, the executive flag certifies and only the harness gate refuses (optimality measure $0.480$). The Fable session reproduced the case (\file{fo2s_margin_certificate_1118.md}): MOSEK solves this family only to a relative primal violation of $10^{-6}$ to $4\times10^{-6}$, and with $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{neg}}=0$ such a violation admits growth rates of order $10^{-4}$; the strict near-optimality rule of MOSEK returns unknown at every step, the stable ones included. Reaches on such families are therefore reliable to about $10^{-3}$ of the margin at best, and this report reads them to $10^{-2}$. The same reproduction found that \code{sossolve} classifies an ILL\_POSED exit of MOSEK as optimal, so the executive certifies with $\mcl P=0$. The harness gate refuses such a solve through its optimality measure of $-1$; the executive flag does not. No row of the studies carries such a certificate. A defect report for the author of the classification is in the note; nothing is patched.
